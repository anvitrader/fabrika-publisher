Fabrika Publisher is a personal publishing tool of Anton Panasenko, a content creator. It lets the owner publish his own short vertical videos to his own TikTok account (@anton.dubai.realestate) from his private content dashboard running on his own computer.

How it works

  1. The owner connects his TikTok account once with Login Kit (Login with TikTok) and approves the requested permissions.
  2. In the dashboard he selects a finished video, writes the caption, sees his TikTok display name and avatar, chooses who can view the video from the privacy options TikTok returns for his account, sets interaction settings (comments, duet, stitch) and the content disclosure if needed.
  3. After he explicitly confirms, the tool uploads the video with the Content Posting API and shows the publishing status returned by TikTok.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other TikTok accounts.
